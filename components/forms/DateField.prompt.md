Date input with a popover calendar.

```jsx
<DateField></DateField>
```

Renders `<s-date-field>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): value, defaultValue, id, disabled, name, details, error, label, required, labelAccessibilityVisibility, placeholder, readOnly, autocomplete, view….
Slots: error, details. Events: blur, change, focus, input, invalid, viewchange.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-date-field>…</s-date-field>`.
