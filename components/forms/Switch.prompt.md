On/off setting that applies immediately.

```jsx
<Switch label="Enable widget"></Switch>
```

Renders `<s-switch>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): value, disabled, accessibilityLabel, checked, defaultChecked, details, error, label, required, name, labelAccessibilityVisibility.
Slots: none. Events: change, input, blur.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-switch label="Enable widget">…</s-switch>`.
