Currency amount input.

```jsx
<MoneyField></MoneyField>
```

Renders `<s-money-field>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): value, id, disabled, details, error, label, required, name, labelAccessibilityVisibility, defaultValue, placeholder, readOnly, autocomplete, max….
Slots: error, details. Events: input, change, focus, blur.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-money-field>…</s-money-field>`.
