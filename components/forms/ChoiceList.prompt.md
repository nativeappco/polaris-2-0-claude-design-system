Group of radio (single) or checkbox (multiple) choices.

```jsx
<ChoiceList></ChoiceList>
```

Renders `<s-choice-list>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): disabled, multiple, details, error, label, name, labelAccessibilityVisibility, values, [internals].
Slots: (default). Events: change, input.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-choice-list>…</s-choice-list>`.
