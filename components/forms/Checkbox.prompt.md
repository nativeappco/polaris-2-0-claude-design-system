Binary choice, or one of several independent choices.

```jsx
<Checkbox label="Show on product pages"></Checkbox>
```

Renders `<s-checkbox>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): indeterminate, defaultIndeterminate, required, labelAccessibilityVisibility, value, disabled, accessibilityLabel, checked, defaultChecked, details, error, label, name.
Slots: error, details. Events: change, input, blur.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-checkbox label="Show on product pages">…</s-checkbox>`.
