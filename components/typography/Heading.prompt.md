Section or content heading; level is inferred from nesting inside s-page/s-section.

```jsx
<Heading>Details</Heading>
```

Renders `<s-heading>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): fontSize, accessibilityRole, accessibilityVisibility, lineClamp.
Slots: (default). Events: none.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-heading>…</s-heading>`.
