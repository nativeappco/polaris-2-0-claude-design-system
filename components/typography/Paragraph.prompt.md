Block of body text.

```jsx
<Paragraph>Bundles are sold as a single product.</Paragraph>
```

Renders `<s-paragraph>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): color, tone, fontSize, fontWeight, fontVariantNumeric, accessibilityVisibility, dir, lineClamp.
Slots: (default). Events: none.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-paragraph>…</s-paragraph>`.
