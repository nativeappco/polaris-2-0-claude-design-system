Groups related content with an optional heading and actions; the Polaris 2.0 replacement for cards.

```jsx
<Section heading="Details">…</Section>
```

Renders `<s-section>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): accessibilityLabel, heading, subheading, padding.
Slots: (default), primary-action, secondary-actions, graphic, accessory, supplemental. Events: none.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-section heading="Details">…</s-section>`.
