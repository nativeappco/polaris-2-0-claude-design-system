Responsive image with aspect ratio and object-fit.

```jsx
<Image></Image>
```

Renders `<s-image>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): loading, accessibilityRole, inlineSize, aspectRatio, objectFit, src, alt, srcSet, sizes, border, borderColor, borderRadius, borderStyle, borderWidth.
Slots: none. Events: error, load.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-image>…</s-image>`.
