Small square preview of a product or file image.

```jsx
<Thumbnail size="small" src="…" alt="Gift set"></Thumbnail>
```

Renders `<s-thumbnail>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): size, src, alt.
Slots: none. Events: load, error.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-thumbnail size="small" src="…" alt="Gift set">…</s-thumbnail>`.
