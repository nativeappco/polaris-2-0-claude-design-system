Unstyled interactive container that makes a region of content clickable (row-style navigation).

```jsx
<Clickable></Clickable>
```

Renders `<s-clickable>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): loading, lang, disabled, background, border, borderWidth, borderStyle, borderColor, borderRadius, padding, paddingBlock, paddingBlockStart, paddingBlockEnd, paddingInline….
Slots: (default). Events: click, focus, blur.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-clickable>…</s-clickable>`.
