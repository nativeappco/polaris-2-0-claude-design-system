Toggle button with an on/off pressed state, for view or formatting toggles.

```jsx
<PressButton></PressButton>
```

Renders `<s-press-button>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): tone, icon, variant, disabled, accessibilityLabel, lang, inlineSize, loading, pressed, defaultPressed.
Slots: (default). Events: click, focus, blur.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-press-button>…</s-press-button>`.
