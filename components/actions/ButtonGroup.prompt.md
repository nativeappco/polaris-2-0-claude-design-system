Groups related buttons with consistent spacing; supports primary-action and secondary-actions slots.

```jsx
<ButtonGroup></ButtonGroup>
```

Renders `<s-button-group>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): accessibilityLabel, gap.
Slots: (default), primary-action, secondary-actions. Events: none.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-button-group>…</s-button-group>`.
