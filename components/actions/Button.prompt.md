Primary way to trigger an action or navigate; one primary per page or section.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" icon="plus">Create bundle</Button>
```

Renders `<s-button>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): tone, icon, accessibilityLabel, variant, lang, type, interestFor, disabled, download, target, command, commandFor, href, inlineSize….
Slots: (default). Events: click, focus, blur.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-button variant="primary" icon="plus">…</s-button>`.
