Interactive chip that acts as a link or button (filters, tags).

```jsx
<ClickableChip>Active</ClickableChip>
```

Renders `<s-clickable-chip>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): hidden, disabled, href, removable, color, interestFor, accessibilityLabel, command, commandFor.
Slots: (default), graphic. Events: click, remove, afterhide.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-clickable-chip>…</s-clickable-chip>`.
