Floating container anchored to a trigger (menus, pickers).

```jsx
<Popover></Popover>
```

Renders `<s-popover>`. Props (camelCase → lowercase attribute): blockSize, inlineSize, maxBlockSize, maxInlineSize, minBlockSize, minInlineSize.
Slots: (default). Events: hide, show, afterhide, aftershow, toggle, aftertoggle.
Plain-HTML equivalent (preferred in production): `<s-popover>…</s-popover>`.
